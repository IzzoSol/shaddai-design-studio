*Written by QUILL for $ASIMOV. Original animation. 3:04, 1920×1080, 30 fps.*

**Logline.** Every cat has nine lives. Ours was there for each leap, from the first spark to the GPU,
and on the night a robot chose a human over an order.

**Through-line.** Asimov the cat is the witness. Each chapter opens on a "LIFE" card, and the cat sits
in every frame of history. The robot story (the Meridian Collider and R-7's First Law choice) is the
spine. The cat's lives cut into it at the moments they belong.
