$Asimov's Laws of Tokenomics

In 1942, Isaac Asimov wrote three laws to keep robots from hurting people. These seven are meant to keep holders from hurting themselves.

  1. A holder may not ape in with money they cannot afford to lose or, through inaction, skip reading the contract address.Only trust the CA posted on this page and the official X account
  2. A holder must obey the chart, except where the chart was drawn by a stranger in your DMs.No team member will ever message you first
  3. A wallet must protect its own existence, as long as that does not mean typing its seed phrase into any website.Nobody legitimate will ever ask for your seed phrase
  4. A robot must never confuse a meme token with a share of NVIDIA.$ASIMOV trades against NVDAx. Only NVDAx is backed by shares, 1:1
  5. Every price on this page must come from a live source, or not be shown at all.Live prices load from DexScreener
  6. A token page may promise jokes, robots and good books, and nothing else.No roadmap of moons. Not financial advice
  7. Before buying the token, a holder should consider buying the book.See "Read the originals" below
